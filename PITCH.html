2 of 4  ·  Problem & solution

Claims fraud leaks billions. We triage every claim in seconds, with the evidence attached.

The problem
  • Leakage on genuine-looking claims - staged accidents, inflated bills, reused photos, duplicates
  • Investigators are overloaded - real fraud hides in a queue that gets equal scrutiny
  • AI-faked evidence is here now - fake photos and doctored documents defeat the human eye
  • ~10% of claims carry fraud, and 25-30% of claim media is AI-faked
The solution
  • Four AI agents read the whole file into one case: extract, timeline, fraud, amount
  • Six fraud layers - rules, photo-hashing, ML, similar-claims, red-flags, LLM synthesis
  • Ranked report with cited evidence, a coverage check and a fair-cost estimate
  • Flags and ranks, never auto-denies - a human makes the final call
Recall 1.00: zero missed fraud ~$0.02 per claim Seconds, not days
3 of 4  ·  The market

Start in Pakistan's motor market. Ride a global fraud problem growing double digits.

Why this market, why now
  • $308.6B lost to insurance fraud in the US every year; auto and property-casualty fraud alone is about $45B
  • Fraud-detection software is booming - roughly $7B in 2025 to about $45B by 2034, over 20% CAGR
  • ~$950B in global motor insurance premiums (2025); every carrier and TPA runs a claims queue
  • AI-faked evidence is scaling the problem, and regulators now want explainable decisions
Our wedge: Pakistan first
  • ~$2.4B in motor insurance premiums (2024), growing every year
  • Only 3% of vehicles are insured - huge headroom as enforcement tightens
  • 0.7% penetration of GDP, among the lowest globally, a market about to expand
  • Land, then expand - South Asia and the GCC, then health and property claims
